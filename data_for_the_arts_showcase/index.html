    04Process

    1. Collect

      Images come from open sources: Wikimedia Commons, iNaturalist and the Wellcome Collection. Each one keeps its author, license and link.

    2. Cut out

      A segmentation model removes the background. One click can pick an organ out of a jar or a dish. The organ is placed on a single neutral ground.

    3. Mask

      The outline becomes a map with one class: animal, plant, fungal, metaphor or scifi. This is the only thing the model will be given.

    4. Review

      Automatic checks drop frames where the subject is tiny, cropped or split in pieces. A person keeps or rejects what is left.

    5. Learn

      Planned, not done: a small mask-to-image network that answers a painted region with a specimen.
